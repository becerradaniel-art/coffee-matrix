          How the numbers are worked out
          Differential
          The premium (+) or discount (−) to the ICE “C” Arabica futures price, in US cents per pound, exactly as given in the Diff columns of the matrix.
          Best and lowest performing
          The grades with the highest and lowest differential among the coffees currently selected. When several grades share the lowest or highest value, the tile says how many.
          Average and trend
          The average is a plain mean of every selected grade’s differential. The change compares like for like: only grades quoted in both this matrix and the one before it.
          Regions
          The matrix’s LATAM and CAM columns make up Latin America & the Caribbean, Africa makes up Africa & the Middle East, and APAC makes up Asia & Pacific Islands.
          Matrix
          Differentials run down the side in 10 ¢/lb bands, and each region, origin and process (W washed, SD sun-dried) has a column, in the spreadsheet’s order. Each grade sits in the band its differential falls in, so you can compare across origins at a glance.
          Colour
          Green above New York, red below, slate at par. Each side runs to its own extreme in view, so the brightest green is the highest differential and the brightest red the lowest. It rescales when you filter. A matrix cell holding several grades takes the colour of their average.
          Map
          Each origin is shaded by its top grade’s differential, or by the average of its grades. Small origins also get a dot. Click an origin to filter the dashboard to it.
